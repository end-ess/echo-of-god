Skills

Devour: Instead of a normal attack, pick one adjacent non-giant unit that can act (either side) and swallow it (it leaves the board, cannot be targeted; only one at a time). After Taotie's next active move, disgorge it onto any empty cell around Taotie; if it is an enemy deity, deal damage to it. If a creation is picked, it is digested at once and Taotie gains 2 HP. Belly Ache: While an enemy deity is inside, Taotie takes damage equal to that deity's damage at the end of each of its own side's action phases. When Taotie dies, the swallowed deity is disgorged where it stood.

Quote
